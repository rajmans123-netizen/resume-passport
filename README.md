# Exact Resume

Fill in your resume once and get it in the format each country commonly expects: paper size, length, photo, personal details, date style and section names.

Free PDF with real, selectable text. No sign-up, no payment, no watermark. Everything runs in your browser, and your details are saved only on your device.

Live: https://rajmans123-netizen.github.io/exact-resume/

**Countries:** India (private and government or PSU), USA, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Germany, Switzerland, UAE, Singapore.

The formats are common practice from career services and recruiters, not laws, and advice varies. Sources are listed in `rules.js` and inside the app. If a job ad or notice asks for something else, follow that.
